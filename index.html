AI Email Checker

A personal job-search inbox assistant.

AI Email Checker connects to your Gmail or Outlook account, imports your incoming mail, and uses AI to sort job-search emails into categories such as interview invitations, scheduled interviews, rejections, and offers. It also collects interview dates into a calendar view so nothing gets missed.

How it works

  • You sign in with Google (or Microsoft) and grant access to your mailbox.
  • New mail is delivered to the app in real time and classified automatically.
  • The app runs on your own computer; your mail is stored in a local database you control.

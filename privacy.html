Privacy Policy

AI Email Checker · Last updated October 3, 2026

What this app accesses

When you connect a Google account, AI Email Checker requests access to your Gmail messages (read, modify labels, and send replies you write in the app) and your basic profile (name and email address). This access is used only to show, organize, classify, and reply to your own email inside the app.

How your data is stored

  • The app runs on the account owner's own computer. Imported messages are stored in a local database on that computer.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to talk to the Gmail API on your behalf.
  • No data is stored on servers operated by the developer.

AI processing

To classify messages, email content may be processed by the AI model selected in the app's settings. This can be a model running locally, or a third-party AI provider (such as OpenAI or Google Gemini) if you choose one. Content is sent only for the purpose of classifying your email and extracting interview details.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except the AI provider you select for classification as described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI Email Checker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can disconnect an account in the app at any time, which stops syncing and deletes its stored messages. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
